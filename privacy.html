CuMeal Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 25, 2026

CuMeal ("CuMeal", "we", "us", or "our") is a mobile application that provides CU mess menu information for today and tomorrow and displays mess timings.

Information we collect

CuMeal does not require an account or login and does not ask you to provide your name, email address, phone number, location, or other personal information directly in the app.

Analytics

CuMeal uses Firebase Analytics to understand how the app is used, such as which screens and app features are used. Firebase Analytics is provided by Google and may collect usage, device, and diagnostic information according to Google's applicable documentation and privacy practices.

Menu data

CuMeal retrieves menu information from Firebase Realtime Database. The database contains menu information such as breakfast, lunch, snacks, and dinner entries. This menu content is not submitted by individual users through the app.

Local storage

CuMeal stores menu information locally on your device so previously retrieved menus can be displayed without another database request. This cached menu information is not an account or user profile.

Advertising

The current version of CuMeal does not display advertising.

Location

CuMeal does not request or use your device's location.

Data sharing

CuMeal does not sell your personal information. The app uses third-party services, including Google Firebase services, to provide analytics and database functionality. Information handled by those services is subject to their applicable terms and privacy policies.

Data retention and deletion

Menu data cached on your device can be removed by deleting the CuMeal app or clearing its app data using the operating system's available controls. CuMeal does not maintain a user account or user profile that you can delete from within the app.

Children

CuMeal is not specifically directed to children and does not knowingly request personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app's data practices change or when necessary to reflect applicable requirements. The updated policy will be published on this page with a new effective date.
