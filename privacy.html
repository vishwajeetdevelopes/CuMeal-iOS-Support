CuMeal is designed to be a simple mess-menu utility. We collect information needed to provide account features and understand general app usage. We do not request your device location and the current app does not display advertising.

01

Overview

This Privacy Policy applies to CuMeal - CU Mess Menu and explains how Vishwajeet Kumar ("we", "us", or "our") handles information associated with the app.

By using CuMeal, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with this policy, please discontinue use of the app.

02

Information We Collect

Account information

If you create or use a CuMeal account, we may process information associated with your account, such as your email address, display name, profile information provided by an authentication provider, and the unique identifier assigned to your account.

Information from sign-in providers

CuMeal supports Google Sign-In, Sign in with Apple, and email-based authentication. Depending on the sign-in method you choose, the authentication provider may provide information such as your name, email address, provider-specific account identifier, or profile information.

Sign in with Apple may provide an Apple private relay email address instead of your personal email address if you choose Apple's private-email option.

Usage information

CuMeal uses Firebase Analytics to understand how the app is used. This may include information about app interactions, screens or features used, approximate technical information about the device, and other analytics information made available by the analytics service.

Information you voluntarily send to us

If you use the in-app Support option and send an email to us, we receive the information contained in that email, including the email address used to send it and any details you choose to provide.

Information we do not intentionally collect

  • We do not request or use your device's precise location.
  • We do not require your phone number to create a CuMeal account.
  • We do not ask users to submit mess-menu content through the app.
  • The current version of CuMeal does not display advertising.
03

Account and Login

CuMeal uses authentication services to allow users to sign in and maintain an account. Authentication is handled through Firebase Authentication and supported sign-in providers.

Depending on the sign-in method you use, Google, Apple, or email authentication may provide or require different account details. We only use the information made available through the selected authentication method for purposes related to providing your CuMeal account and service.

CuMeal does not receive your Google or Apple password. Passwords and authentication credentials are handled by the applicable authentication system rather than being directly accessible to the CuMeal developer.

You are responsible for keeping access to your account and your device secure. If you believe your account has been accessed without authorization, please contact us.

04

Support Emails

CuMeal includes an in-app Support tab. Selecting it can open the default mail application on your device with a message addressed to our support email.

Opening the mail application does not itself send us anything. You decide whether to review, modify, and send the email.

When you send a support email, we may receive your email address, the contents of your message, and any attachments or other information you voluntarily include.

We use support communications to respond to requests, troubleshoot problems, improve the app, and maintain the service. Please do not include passwords, one-time authentication codes, payment-card information, or other sensitive information that is not necessary for your support request.

05

How We Use Information

We may use information associated with CuMeal to:

  • Create, maintain, and authenticate your account.
  • Provide the features and functionality of the app.
  • Store and associate account-related information with your CuMeal account.
  • Respond to support requests and communications.
  • Understand app usage and improve performance and user experience.
  • Maintain the security, reliability, and integrity of the service.
  • Detect, prevent, or investigate misuse, fraud, or technical problems.
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations and respond to lawful requests.

We do not use your information for advertising personalization in the current version of CuMeal.

06

Sharing and Third-Party Providers

We do not sell your personal information. We may share or make information available to service providers that help us operate CuMeal, where necessary for the purposes described in this policy.

These providers may include:

  • Google Firebase, for authentication, analytics, and database functionality.
  • Google, when you choose Google Sign-In.
  • Apple, when you choose Sign in with Apple.
  • Your email service provider when you voluntarily send us a support email.

Third-party providers process information according to their own privacy policies and applicable terms. We do not control how an independent provider operates its services.

07

Firebase Services

CuMeal uses services provided by Google Firebase. These services currently include:

Firebase Authentication

Firebase Authentication is used to manage user sign-in and account authentication for supported login methods.

Firebase Analytics

Firebase Analytics is used to understand general app usage and improve the product. Analytics information may include app events, interactions, and technical information made available to the analytics service.

Firebase Realtime Database

Firebase Realtime Database is used to provide and store the menu information used by CuMeal. Menu information is application data rather than content submitted by individual users.

Firebase may process information in accordance with Google's applicable privacy practices and service terms.

08

Local Storage and Caching

CuMeal may cache menu information locally on your device so that relevant menu data can load more efficiently and the app can provide a smoother experience.

Local cached menu information is not intended to be a user submission and is separate from your account credentials.

09

Data Retention

We retain account and other personal information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, maintain security, respond to requests, comply with applicable legal obligations, and resolve disputes.

Analytics information may be retained according to the applicable configuration and retention practices of the analytics service.

Support emails may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to manage support, maintain records, improve the service, or comply with legal obligations.

10

Account Deletion and Your Choices

You may stop using CuMeal at any time. Where account deletion is available within the app, you may use the provided option to request deletion of your CuMeal account.

You may also contact us at vishwajeetx14@gmail.com if you want to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information associated with your account.

When an account deletion request is processed, we will take reasonable steps to delete associated personal information, subject to information we are required or permitted to retain under applicable law, legitimate security requirements, or other lawful purposes.

Deleting the app from your device does not necessarily delete your CuMeal account. Use the available account-deletion option or contact us if you want your account removed.

11

Security

We take reasonable measures designed to protect information handled by CuMeal. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Account authentication and infrastructure security also depend on the security controls provided by third-party services used by the app.

12

CuMeal is not directed toward children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

If you believe that a child under 13 has provided personal information to us, please contact us. If we become aware of such information, we will take reasonable steps to address it.

When we make material changes, we may update the "Last updated" date and, where appropriate, provide additional notice through the app or other reasonable means.

Your continued use of CuMeal after an updated policy becomes effective means the updated policy will apply to your use of the service, to the extent permitted by applicable law.
