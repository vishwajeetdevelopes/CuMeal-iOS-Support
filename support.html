Common help

  • Menu not available: A menu may be unavailable when no menu has been published for that date.
  • Outdated menu: CuMeal caches menu information on the device. If a cached menu is available, it is shown without another database request.
  • Other issues: Include your iPhone model, iOS version, and a description or screenshot when contacting support.
